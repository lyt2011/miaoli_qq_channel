# v0.12.0 - 2026-11-25

## Added

- Fail-closed group allowlist; private chats remain unrestricted except bot self-echo suppression.
- Group addressing controls, alias matching, probability triggers, and bounded follow-up windows.
- Recent-message context summaries and automatic group reply length limit.
- Isolated policy and backlog tests.

## Changed

- `plugins/meme_ops/nodes/inject_memes.py`: passes `get_thread_id()` into `inject_human`, matching the per-session injection table in `miaoli_bot` 0.59.0.
- Minimum `miaoli_bot` raised to `>=0.59.0` (`metadata.toml` / `manifest.toml` / `README.md`); on 0.58.x the two-argument call raises `TypeError`.

## Notes

- Group whitelist defaults to empty; configure allowed group IDs after upgrading.
- Follow-up windows are in-memory and reset on restart.
- The channel cannot suppress main-package LLM execution or restrict messages sent through separate QQ tools.
